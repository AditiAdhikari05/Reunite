Solution flowchart

From report to reunion

  1. Report arrivesA family reports someone missing, or staff or the public report someone found
  2. Read the textGranite pulls out hair, clothing and marks, keeping only words in the reportGenerative AI · chip
  3. Score every pairThe model ranks likely matches with a score and reasonsPredictive AI
  4. Staff reviewA trained person confirms or rejects each suggestion
  5. Family sees statusSearching, Possible match, or Reunited. Never other people's details
LinuxONE capabilities used

Why LinuxONE

Compute

Telum on-chip AI accelerator: MiniLM 9.4× and Granite 6.9× faster than the CPU, same output

Security

Sensitive reports stay on one server. No cloud AI API. Role-based access, hashed passwords

Containerization

The app, models and data run in a Docker container on LinuxONE Community Cloud

Scalability

All report pairs are scored in one batch: 235,025 pairs in 0.4 s
